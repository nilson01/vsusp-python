# Attribution

The selection implementations adapt VsusP R source by Nilson Chapagain and
Debdeep Pati, distributed under GPL (>= 3). This Python package is distributed
under GPL-3.0-or-later. It is provided without warranty; see LICENSE.

S2M and basic 2-M follow Li and Pati (2017),
https://doi.org/10.1016/j.csda.2016.10.008.
SAVS follows Ray and Bhattacharya (2018), https://arxiv.org/abs/1810.09004.
The DSS adaptive-L1 approximation follows Hahn and Carvalho (2015),
https://doi.org/10.1080/01621459.2014.993077.

Gaussian sampling uses the half-Cauchy auxiliary representation of Makalic and
Schmidt (2016), https://arxiv.org/abs/1508.03884, and the Gaussian sampling
identity of Bhattacharya, Chakraborty and Mallick (2016),
https://doi.org/10.1093/biomet/asw042. The variance prior and flat-intercept
convention are documented in docs/methods.md.

Figure development used Scientific Agent Skills: Kassis, T., Agarwal, V.,
He, Y., Patel, D., and Brueckner, A. M. (2026).
https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2609.00065.
